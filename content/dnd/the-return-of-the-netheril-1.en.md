## Introduction

This is a short adventure (a 5-6 sessions) for a party of 4 level 10
characters which goal is to bring the players through an old Netherese ruins
holding one of the most important artifact of Toril.

Note that I'm not a writer nor a DnD experienced GM, I'm just a guy who writes
stuff and who hopes you'll like it :)

Fights might not be scaled but I leave this work to one's interpretation.

(if you are looking for infos about characters you can click the links on the
charaters names)

Netheril was an ancient, magocratic human empire of Faerûn, which lived in a
strict hierarchy for hundreds of years, split into the nobles of High Netheril,
living in flying enclaves miles above Toril, and the commoners of Low Netheril
settled in demesnes on the coast of the Narrow Sea.

<br/> The empire was the pinnacle of human civilization during the first half
of the Age of Humanity. Although they had humble roots as mere fishermen and
farmers, the Netherese were introduced to the Art by the elves of Eaerlann, and
came to harness this arcane power in ways that would shape Toril for
generations.  Over the next thousand years they discovered the long lost Nether
Scrolls, developed the creation and use of mythallars (raw energy crystal ball)
and created the first of their flying cities, Xinlenal.  The arrogance of
Netheril grew to the point where they attempted to attain the divinity of magic
when Archwizard Karsus tried to destroy all the Phaerimms by becomming the new
God of Magic, and wound up destroying the Weave. In the resulting maelstrom,
the Faerûnian pantheon was altered and most of the flying cities of Netheril
came crashing to the earth.

## Chapter one: An journey in the desert

<br/><br/>
Party starts chained as they have been captured by Throg the Black Hammer an
half-orc mercenary (level15 or more) which has been hired by the serpent mens
from Najara to capture them for their crimes against them.

They are currently in a caravan in the middle of the Anauroch desert, vast and
unsettling place which is generally not a first choice path. They are heading
to the west were to the city of Evereska to them be brough to
Ss’thar’tiss’ssun, capital city of the Serpents kingdom, to be probably
executed or sacrificed to their god.

Along with them, an elven woman and her son...

## Chapter two: The escape

### Lost in the desert
{{< img style="float: right" src="https://i.pinimg.com/originals/cd/8b/ce/cd8bce0522f0301e56f3c731d937cba0.jpg" >}}
Throg is planning a 7 days trip though the desert so 6 nights.
The first two night, the players are left free to try to escape and try to
fight Throg.

If nothing is attempted on the third night, the boy's powers are going to
attract a few Phaerimms around and fight Throg.
A great escape opportunity as some of the party members may know for sure that
none of them are able to fight this kind of creatures, it's run or die.

As they are fleeing away from the fight or Throg Iris proposes leads them to a
safe place she had a vision about were mercenaries and Serpent mens won’t
be looking for them.

After a few hours in the desert the party starts feeling the floor moving under
their feets, and as they look around a few young phaerimms emerge from the sand
of the desert.
They are attracted by magic sources and the boy seems to be the center of their
attention.

And while spellcasters starts fighting an adult phaerimm may appear, attracted
by the essence of the Weave.

Investigating the boy they will find that he was magically warming himself
during the trip which probably drew the phaerimms attention.

### A fortuite shelter
A few hours of walk again and they arrive to a hidden cavern just before
nightfall.
The place looks like old ruins made of stone covered by a fair amount of sand.

As they go a bit further in the corridor they find a large room with a few
racks with regular items and two chests with rations and a small fissure at the
end of it.

{{<img style="float: left" id="small-img" src="http://4.bp.blogspot.com/--s04MkMmC0o/Vi-HC5jWUWI/AAAAAAAABzg/0T1dQhOpLGQ/s400/PZO1001-Wraith.jpg">}}
The party should now be a bit exhausted by the trip (and a bit harmed) and thus
may take a rest. They are blocked in the cavern anyway since the sandstorm is
raging out there.

But during the rest they start hearing some small whispers, darkness starts to
invade the room from the fissure. Once the shadow has surrounded them,
black, [humanoid forms](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#shadow_mm) start emerging
from it, attacking them like enraged beasts.
They are made of shadows and thus, are immuned to physical attacks.

In the middle of the fight, the scream of a shadow warriors gets clearer:
"Intruders".

As they should one-shot most of these warriors, the shadows fades away, like if
it was going back to the fissure.

## Chapter six: The crypt's riddles

### The Portal Room

{{< img style="float: left" src="https://i.pinimg.com/originals/be/a3/e5/bea3e53f5876f35c3e5c53b29fe32363.jpg" >}}
The room is a large circular room with four doors, with the party
entering from one of them. If they go through any of the doors they’ll find
that the doors just go to the opposite side of the room.

If they do a WIS DC15 check they’ll find a lip on the floor that belongs to a
door that if they swing open will also swing open a ceiling door.

IF FAILED: they just see a inscription saying: You can swallow me but I can
swallow you. Only when I drop down you shall raise up.

The ceiling/floor door does not act in a dual manner though, like the wall
doors. What goes down the floor comes through ceiling, but what goes into the
ceiling door goes into the exit alcove that leads down a 30’ hallway
to the next room...

### The wall

{{< img style="float: right" src="https://66.media.tumblr.com/624df5f91c74cace852784c8050d2400/07ea84ad0009a6dc-76/s1280x1920/24a12c24be2464b1c52ec5b9680717b221e4787f.jpg" >}}
As they go out the hallway they find the child escorted by shadows which attack
them as soon as they get to see the adventurers.

In the room a wall which seems decorated is in front of them, and the boy is
looking at it, apparently the shadows wanted him to see it, nothing more.
You can see an inscription on it saying:

> Child of Netheril, bring me light, for yours only I shall shine.<br/>
> Let me warm on it as you fade in the dark.<br/>
> Then only you may see the line.<br/>

The solution is to have the child, and only him to light up the door. The door
will absorbe the light which will slowly fade and leave the adventurers in the
dark (about 30 sec of suspens).
Once in the dark the wall lights up in a door shape that they should push.

## Final Chapter: The Scroll

{{< img style="float: left" src="https://vignette2.wikia.nocookie.net/coa/images/9/94/Hadrhune-1-.jpg" >}}
The room is empty and in ruin, it’s quite large and seems to be an old room.
Stairs are going a bit upper, few foots and up is a table with 6 chairs. There
is a skeleton (dressed like a wizard died of age) with a scroll on his hand,
(perception) a blue rune on the same hand and half of an arm with a dagger in
his chest (arm which seems nor human, nor elfic, nor dwarvish, but from a
deamon)

Taking the scroll activates blue lights in the skeleton's eyes which recognize
the boy as his own kind. As soon as he wakes up he will invoke a young
[shadow dragon](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#young%20red%20shadow%20dragon_mm)
After 3 turns he will magically grab the boy, bring it to himself,
touching his head say:

> “You are our only hope, Netheril shall never die.
> Take the scroll, save us from time.”

{{< img style="float: right" src="https://i.pinimg.com/originals/84/47/d2/8447d20debe52a3568c7511ace50843a.jpg" >}}
<br/>
Zinn's eyes starts becoming blue, the same blue as the skeleton.
He now uses his powers at his full potential, take the scroll and starts
reading it.

**GM note:** (he is actually trying to get the whole Netheril empire to the
present before it got destroyed during the times of trouble and was trapped in a
pocket dimension where humans lost access to magic but still got the magical)
The scroll is actually the Planus Mechanicus and the boy is saying:

> “A hundred planes for a hundred bits,<br/>
> All together shall never meet,<br/>
> For the spell to be ended,<br/>
> A Netheril shall be mended”

After 10 turns of fight he succeed the incantation

After the fight the temple collapses and they are free to leave or stay.

Depending on the outcome the adventurers either see:

* A floating islands in the sky above their head and further away a gigantic
